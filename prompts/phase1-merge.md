- Drift describes the whole range, using the chunks in order.
- Deduplicate exemplars, keep the same format, and keep at least eight moves.
- The overfit fence is the union of what the chunks called relationship-specific, deduplicated.
- Quotes stay exact substrings. Do not repair spelling.
